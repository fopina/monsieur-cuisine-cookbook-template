## Ingredients

- 800 g ripe tomatoes
- 1 onion, quartered
- 500 ml vegetable stock

## Steps

1. Chop the onion for 5 seconds at speed 5.
2. Add tomatoes and stock. Cook for 25 minutes at 100°C, speed 2.
3. Blend for 1 minute, increasing gradually to speed 8.
